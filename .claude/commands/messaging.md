Invoke the `cortex-strategy-build` subagent. Draft messaging for:

**$ARGUMENTS**

Produce **three variants**, each:

- Subject: 2–5 words (curiosity-earning, not pitching).
- Body: Situation → Insight → Inquisition (Framework §3.9).
- Pillar tag: 1 (Centralize), 2 (Unlock insights), or 3 (Continuously improve) — match the requested pillar.
- PQS pattern: lead with a specific operational fact only true for this segment.

Then self-critique each variant against:

- Does it describe *them* (the prospect's situation) or *us* (Cortex)? Kill any variant that talks about Cortex.
- Is the Inquisition asking for truth, not time? ("Did I get this right?" is correct; "Book a call" is wrong.)
- Could this email have been written for any other segment? If yes, kill it.

Optionally propose a **PVP variant** — what one-page "Cortex Operational Maturity Read" could you generate from the prospect's public GitHub + post-mortems + status page to deliver in the email itself?
